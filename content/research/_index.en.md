<div class="mb5">
<h3 class="f3 fw6 mb2 dark-gray bb b--light-silver pb2">Autonomous Vehicles and Advanced Driver Assistance Systems (ADAS)</h3>
<p class="f5 lh-copy black-70 mt2 mb3">Leveraging deep learning and sensor fusion, we develop autonomous driving systems capable of reliable operation in complex traffic and adverse weather conditions, with closed-loop validation on real vehicles.</p>
<ul class="pl3 f5 lh-copy dark-gray mt0">
<li class="mb2">Vision Transformer-based multi-modal perception (camera, LiDAR, radar fusion)</li>
<li class="mb2">Robust visual perception under adverse weather conditions (rain and fog)</li>
<li class="mb2">GNSS/INS integrated localization with integrity monitoring (Fault Detection and Exclusion)</li>
<li class="mb2">Steer-by-wire system modeling and Model-in-the-Loop (MIL) simulation</li>
<li class="mb2">High-speed trajectory tracking and lateral dynamic stability control</li>
<li class="mb2">Full-vehicle mechatronic integration and vehicle dynamics control</li>
</ul>
</div>

<div class="mb5">
<h3 class="f3 fw6 mb2 dark-gray bb b--light-silver pb2">Heterogeneous Unmanned Vehicle Swarm Coordination and Control</h3>
<p class="f5 lh-copy black-70 mt2 mb3">We establish a unified collaborative framework for heterogeneous platforms including UGVs, UAVs, and AMRs, achieving efficient multi-agent cooperative missions through semantic communication and reinforcement learning.</p>
<ul class="pl3 f5 lh-copy dark-gray mt0">
<li class="mb2">Heterogeneous platform (UGV, UAV, AMR) cooperative task architecture</li>
<li class="mb2">Deep reinforcement learning-based multi-agent end-to-end path planning</li>
<li class="mb2">CARLA + SUMO joint traffic simulation and validation platform</li>
<li class="mb2">Vehicle-to-Everything (V2X) and cloud/edge collaborative resource scheduling</li>
</ul>
</div>

<div class="mb4">
<h3 class="f3 fw6 mb2 dark-gray bb b--light-silver pb2">UAV Systems and Flight Controller Anti-Interference Research</h3>
<p class="f5 lh-copy black-70 mt2 mb3">Targeting complex electromagnetic environments and GNSS-denied scenarios, we research high-precision inertial navigation, hybrid propulsion systems, and swarm flight control technologies.</p>
<ul class="pl3 f5 lh-copy dark-gray mt0">
<li class="mb2">Fiber Optic Gyroscope (FOG) inertial navigation system development and high-precision localization</li>
<li class="mb2">Distributed UAV swarm formation control algorithms</li>
<li class="mb2">Anti-jamming and backup localization mechanisms in GPS-denied environments</li>
<li class="mb2">Hybrid-electric propulsion system architecture and Hardware-in-the-Loop (HIL) testing</li>
</ul>
</div>
